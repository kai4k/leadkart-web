Your company runs on one operating system. Which world should it live in?

Every module is an app. Home is yours to arrange. Search jumps anywhere, and every record links to the records around it. That part never changes. What changes is the belief behind the interface: below are 1 complete worlds, each with its own layout, material, type, shapes and behaviour, each shown as a full product demo.

  • Home you define
  • Apps with names, one click away
  • ⌘K jumps anywhere
  • Records link to records
  • Installs on the phone

Each world below has five parts: Philosophy, OS shell, Apps, Components and Mobile PWA. The desk in each one is live — click its switcher, search, bell and create button. When one feels like yours, press its Choose button.
